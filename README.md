# 🏋️‍♂️ 10-Day Rotational Fitness Tracker

A mobile-first, zero-setup Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for middle-aged trainees balancing joint health, longevity, conditioning, and busy schedules. 

Unlike traditional 7-day splits tied to specific days of the week, this application uses a **10-Workout "Next Up" Rotator**. This allows for flexible rest days and unpredictable schedules without ever missing a session or losing training momentum.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🔄 10-Workout Full-Body Rotation:** Keeps every major muscle group stimulated every 48–72 hours while alternating primary movement patterns.
* **📍 Home vs. Work Gym Toggle:** Dynamically swaps exercises based on available equipment:
  * **Home Setup:** Kettlebells, resistance bands, pull-up bar, jump rope, plyo box, air bike.
  * **Work Gym Setup:** Leg extension/curl, cable machine, lat pulldown, chest machines, sleds, treadmills.
* **🩺 Joint Prehab & Daily Check-In:**
  * Interactive **0–10 Knee & Shoulder Pain Scales**.
  * Dynamic warning banners with joint-sparing tips based on pain levels.
  * Integrated daily prehab checklists targeting meniscus recovery (TKEs, Spanish squats) and shoulder mobility (band pull-aparts, thoracic openers).
* **🔀 Smart Exercise Swapper:** Instantly replace any movement with an alternative muscle-equivalent exercise tailored to shoulder or knee safety.
* **⏱️ Density Circuit & Zone 2 Timers:** Built-in stopwatch for density circuits and a dedicated Zone 2 steady-state cardio timer.
* **💾 Local Data Persistence:** Automatically saves your workout progress, current rotation index, and exercise weight/rep logs directly in your browser (`localStorage`).
* **📱 Mobile-First / Pixel Optimized:** Styled with clean dark-mode UI and large touch targets designed for quick logging during workouts.
